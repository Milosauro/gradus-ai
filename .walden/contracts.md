## Coherence Review

### agent-compilers
Consistent with `runtime-guard#R2.AC1` and `runtime-guard#C3`. `agent-compilers#R2.AC2` and `agent-compilers#R3.AC3` emit static `PreToolUse` (`gradusctl guard`) dispatchers in `.claude/settings.json` and `.agents/hooks.json` without embedding user rules (ADR 0003), matching `runtime-guard`'s static-dispatcher contract and anti-tamper protection. No inconsistencies found.

### runtime-guard
Consistent with `agent-compilers#R2.AC2`, `agent-compilers#R3.AC3`, and `resolve-and-lock#R2.AC2`. `runtime-guard#R2.AC1` protects `.claude/settings.json` and `.agents/hooks.json` generated by `agent-compilers` with an `ask` anti-tamper floor, while `runtime-guard#R3.AC1` evaluates `~/.gradus/levels/user` locally offline without mutating shared hook files. No inconsistencies found.

### verify-check-evidence
Consistent with `resolve-and-lock#R2.AC2` and `agent-compilers#R4.AC1`. `verify-check-evidence#R1.AC1` and `verify-check-evidence#R1.AC2` reuse `PlanCompile` for byte-exact drift verification and uphold D-73 (`0`/`1`/`2`) exit code semantics across `verify`, `check`, `evidence`, and `hooks`. No inconsistencies found.
